Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2024

Document Version: 2.0 | Effective Date: June 15, 2024

Liuyang Kangfan Trading Co., Ltd. ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our mobile applications, or engage with our services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and postal address
  • Company information and business details
  • Payment information and billing details
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Communication preferences and contact history

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website or use our mobile applications, we automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, browser type)
  • IP address and location data
  • Usage patterns and navigation history
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • App performance data and crash reports

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving our trading services
  • Processing transactions and managing accounts
  • Communicating with you about products and services
  • Personalizing your experience and recommendations
  • Ensuring security and preventing fraud
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Operating and improving our mobile applications

3. Mobile Application and Advertising

3.1 Mobile Application Overview

Our mobile applications are available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store. These applications provide business management tools including inventory tracking, order management, and analytics.

3.2 Advertising and Ad Networks

We integrate various advertising networks and platforms to support our free application offerings. These ad networks may collect user data and display targeted advertisements based on your interests and usage patterns.

3.3 Supported Advertising Platforms

Our mobile applications may integrate with the following advertising and monetization platforms:

  • Google AdMob - Google's mobile advertising platform for app monetization
  • Facebook Audience Network - Facebook's mobile ad network for reaching users across apps and websites
  • Unity Ads - Integrated advertising solution for mobile games and apps
  • AppLovin - Mobile advertising and monetization platform
  • ironSource - Mobile app monetization and user acquisition platform
  • Chartboost - Mobile game advertising and monetization platform
  • Pangle - ByteDance's mobile advertising platform
  • Mintegral - Global mobile advertising platform
  • Vungle - In-app video advertising platform (now part of Digital Turbine)
  • Smaato - Global mobile advertising exchange
  • Mopub - Mobile ad serving platform (now part of AppLovin)
  • InMobi - Mobile advertising and monetization platform
  • Liftoff - Mobile app install and engagement advertising platform
  • Fyber - Mobile app monetization platform
  • StartApp - Mobile advertising platform
  • AdColony - Mobile video advertising platform
  • Verve - Location-based mobile advertising
  • TrialX - Mobile health advertising platform
  • Flurry - Mobile analytics and advertising platform (Yahoo)
  • MoPub - Twitter's mobile ad marketplace

3.4 Types of Advertisements

Our mobile applications may display the following types of advertisements:

  • Splash Ads (Splash Screens) - Full-screen advertisements displayed when the app launches or transitions between screens
  • Rewarded Video Ads - Video advertisements that users can voluntarily watch in exchange for in-app rewards, virtual currency, or premium features
  • Interstitial Ads - Full-screen advertisements displayed at natural transition points in the app, such as between levels or after completing an action
  • Banner Ads - Rectangular advertisements displayed at the top or bottom of the screen, continuously visible during app usage
  • Native Ads - Advertisements designed to match the visual style and format of the app content
  • Playable Ads - Interactive advertisements that allow users to preview app features through a mini-game or demo
  • Offerwall Ads - Advertisements that offer users rewards for completing specific actions or viewing advertisements

3.5 Data Collection by Ad Networks

Advertising networks may collect and process the following types of data:

  • Device identifiers (GAID, IDFA)
  • Device type and technical specifications
  • Operating system and version
  • IP address and approximate geolocation
  • App usage data and in-app behavior
  • Ad interaction history (views, clicks, installs)
  • Installed apps information (for contextual advertising)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

4. Google Play Store Compliance

4.1 Google Play Developer Program Policies

Our mobile applications comply with Google Play Store Developer Program Policies, including:

  • Proper disclosure of data collection and sharing practices
  • Implementation of appropriate security measures
  • Compliance with targeted advertising policies
  • Proper handling of sensitive permissions
  • Transparent in-app billing practices
  • Family-friendly content requirements where applicable

4.2 Google Play Data Safety Section

We provide accurate information in the Google Play Data Safety section, including:

  • Types of data collected (location, personal info, financial info, etc.)
  • Purpose of data collection
  • Whether data is shared with third parties
  • Security practices implemented
  • Options for user control and deletion

5. Apple App Store Compliance

5.1 App Store Review Guidelines

Our iOS applications comply with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, including:

  • Privacy nutrition labels in App Store listings
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework implementation
  • Proper permission requests and justifications
  • Data minimization principles
  • Secure data transmission and storage

5.2 Apple Privacy Nutrition Labels

We accurately declare data practices in Apple Privacy Nutrition Labels for:

  • Data linked to your identity
  • Data not linked to your identity
  • Data used to track you
  • Third-party SDK data practices

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

6.1 Age Restrictions

Our services and applications are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages.

6.2 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

In compliance with COPPA:

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent
  • Parents can review, request deletion of, or refuse further collection of their child's information
  • We provide clear privacy notices and obtain appropriate consent
  • Limited data collection for internal operations only

6.3 GDPR-K Compliance (European Union)

For users in the European Economic Area:

  • We do not knowingly process data of users under 16 years of age without parental consent
  • Member states may set lower age limits (minimum 13 years)
  • We implement age verification mechanisms where required
  • Special protections for children's data under Article 8 GDPR

7. International Data Protection Regulations

7.1 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - European Union

For users in the European Union and European Economic Area:

  • Lawful Basis: We process data based on legitimate interest, contractual necessity, or consent
  • Data Subject Rights: Right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection
  • Data Protection Officer: Contact our DPO for privacy-related inquiries
  • Cross-border Transfers: Appropriate safeguards for international data transfers (Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions)
  • Privacy by Design: We implement privacy considerations throughout our development process
  • Data Breach Notification: 72-hour notification to supervisory authorities for qualifying breaches

7.2 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) - United States

For California residents:

  • Right to Know: Know what personal information is collected, used, and shared
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of exercising privacy rights
  • Financial Incentives: Disclosure of any incentive programs related to data collection

7.3 Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) - Canada

  • Consent-based approach to data collection
  • Individual access to personal information
  • Ability to challenge accuracy and completeness of data
  • Accountability and identification of purposes for data use

7.4 Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) - Brazil

  • Legal bases for data processing
  • Data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, etc.)
  • Data Protection Officer requirement
  • Data breach notification requirements

7.5 Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) - Singapore

  • Consent and notification obligations
  • Purpose limitation principles
  • Access and correction rights
  • Data breach notification requirements

7.6 Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) - China

  • Lawful and necessary data collection
  • Consent for processing sensitive personal information
  • Cross-border data transfer restrictions
  • Data localization requirements for certain data

7.7 Privacy Act 1988 - Australia

  • Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) compliance
  • Open and transparent data handling
  • Anonymity and pseudonymity options where practicable
  • Notifiable data breaches scheme

7.8 Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) - South Africa

  • Lawful processing conditions
  • Special protection for sensitive information
  • Information officer registration and responsibilities
  • Trans-border information flows restrictions

8. Third-Party Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers and business partners
  • Advertising networks and analytics providers
  • Legal and regulatory authorities when required
  • Business transfer parties in case of merger or acquisition

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security assessments and audits
  • Access controls and authentication measures
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Incident response and breach notification procedures

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred across international borders. We ensure appropriate safeguards:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Binding Corporate Rules
  • Adequacy decisions by relevant authorities
  • Certification mechanisms

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal information
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

12. Cookie Policy

We use cookies and similar technologies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic functionality
  • Analytical Cookies: Help us understand usage patterns
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by our advertising partners for targeted ads
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • Email notification to registered users
  • Notice within our mobile applications
  • Updated "Last Updated" date on this page
  • Prominent posting on our website
